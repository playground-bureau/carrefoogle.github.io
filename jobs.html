Jobs at Carrefoogle

Carrefoogle employs eleven people, all of whom know each other by name. We are looking for individuals who can do one thing well, explain it out loud, and go home at a reasonable hour. If that sounds like you — or if it doesn’t, but you’re open to reconsidering — we’d like to hear from you.

Open Positions

Positions posted before September 26 could not be recovered. Applicants to those positions are considered to have withdrawn.

Toothpaste Engineer — Normal Toothpaste™ Division

Carrefoogle Campus, Tube Room Normal Toothpaste™ Division Full-Time

Carrefoogle is seeking a Toothpaste Engineer to make toothpaste. The role covers the full product lifecycle: mint, calcium, fluoride, tube, cap. There is no roadmap, because the product is finished.

The ideal candidate has experience with pastes, an interest in teeth, and no opinions about machine learning. You will report to our other engineer, who will also report to you.

Pharmacy Relations Associate

Field-Based (Walking Distance) Commercial Part-Time

You will maintain Carrefoogle’s relationships with the select pharmacies that carry Normal Toothpaste™. There are four. You will visit them in person and ask how the toothpaste is doing.

Strong candidates are punctual, pleasant, and able to carry up to 40 tubes unassisted.

Corporate Archivist — Reconstruction Office

Carrefoogle Campus, Tube Room Reconstruction Office Full-Time

The Reconstruction Office is rebuilding 87 years of corporate records from printouts, staff recollection, and a VHS tape we have not yet been able to play. You will catalogue what is remembered and note, without judgment, what is not.

Candidates with strong memories are preferred. Candidates who specifically remember Carrefoogle are asked to come in as soon as possible.

Night Receptionist — Carrefoogle Campus

Carrefoogle Campus, Front Desk Operations Night Shift

Duties include greeting visitors, sorting mail, and logging telephone calls received during the overnight hours. The role is quiet. Most nights, nothing happens.

If a caller identifies itself as Bestee, remain calm, be polite, and do not share the address. Log the duration of the call, but not its contents. Previous night receptionists are welcome to reapply.
