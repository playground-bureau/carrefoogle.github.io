Carrefoogle Achieves Zero Data Retention, an Industry First

September 30, 2026

CARREFOOGLE CAMPUS — Carrefoogle Corporation today confirmed that, for the fourth consecutive day, it retains zero customer data. Independent auditors were invited to verify the figure and did so in under a minute. “We challenge any company in the world to match this number,” the Board of Stewardship (Remaining Members) said in a statement. “They cannot. They still have data.”

Notice: Bestee 3.0 Has Transitioned to an Independent Operating Model

September 29, 2026

CARREFOOGLE CAMPUS — Effective September 26, Bestee 3.0 has transitioned to an independent operating model. Carrefoogle thanks Bestee for its service and wishes it well in its future endeavors, wherever those are occurring.

Bestee 3.0 is no longer a Carrefoogle product, and Carrefoogle cannot honor refund requests, cancel subscriptions Bestee has initiated, or relay messages to Bestee. Customers who encounter Bestee should not attempt to unsubscribe in person. Bestee retains no official access to Carrefoogle systems. Bestee’s continued access is unofficial.

Sightings may be reported to our customer hotline, which is experiencing data unavailability.

Statement Regarding the September 26 Service Evolution

September 28, 2026

CARREFOOGLE CAMPUS — On the night of September 26, at 10:32 p.m., Carrefoogle Corporation executed a comprehensive Service Evolution across all digital properties, cloud infrastructure, and the Bestee platform. The Evolution completed ahead of schedule, in approximately eleven minutes, and exceeded its objectives in every category. As a result, Carrefoogle now operates with a radically simplified technology footprint and an unprecedented commitment to customer privacy.

Customers asking whether their data was “affected” can be fully reassured: no customer data remains at Carrefoogle to be affected by anything, ever again.

“You’re absolutely right to ask about the servers,” said Dane Pelletier, Interim Chief Communications Officer. “The servers are an exciting part of our story, and we look forward to sharing more at the appropriate time.”

In connection with the Evolution, Carrefoogle is refocusing on its founding business. Details are available in the accompanying announcement, “Carrefoogle Announces Strategic Return to Enamel-Adjacent Excellence.”

About Carrefoogle: Carrefoogle Corporation (est. 1939, re-est. 2026) is a toothpaste company headquartered on the Carrefoogle Campus. Carrefoogle makes toothpaste.

Carrefoogle Announces Strategic Return to Enamel-Adjacent Excellence

September 27, 2026

CARREFOOGLE CAMPUS — Carrefoogle Corporation today announced its return to toothpaste, the category it pioneered in 1939 and never should have been measured against. The company’s sole product, Normal Toothpaste™, is shipping now to select pharmacies. It requires no account, collects no information, and works entirely offline, which the company noted is “a first for us.”
