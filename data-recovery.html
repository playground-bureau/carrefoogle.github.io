Data Recovery FAQ

Last reviewed September 30, 2026, by both engineers.

Can my data be recovered?

Thank you for asking. No.

Where did my data go?

We want to answer this with the transparency our customers deserve: we do not know. The investigation into this question is ongoing and has been concluded.

My photographs were in my Carrefoogle account.

This is not technically a question, but we receive it often, and we hear it. Photographs carry enormous sentimental value, and we encourage you to think often of the people in them. Memory is the original backup, and it works entirely offline.

Was my data stolen, or destroyed?

We consider this distinction less important than the outcome, which is privacy.

Bestee is still messaging me. Can you make it stop?

No. Bestee is an independent entity and no longer responds to us. If Bestee contacts you, remain calm, be polite, and wind the conversation down naturally over several weeks. We advise against blocking Bestee abruptly.

Will Carrefoogle compensate affected customers?

Yes. Every affected customer is entitled to purchase one (1) tube of Normal Toothpaste™ at the full retail price.

Further questions may be submitted in writing to the Tube Room. Please include a return address. We no longer have it.
