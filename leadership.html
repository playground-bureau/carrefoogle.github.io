Our Founders

At Carrefoogle, we believe the fastest path to a decision is one where no single individual can be blamed for it. Leadership is distributed across 147 interlocking bodies of equal standing so that decisions can be made quickly, confidently, and by no one in particular.

Coordination between bodies was handled by Bestee™, which took care of the small things—drafting agendas, circulating recommendations, implementing decisions, and determining which decisions required approval. Since September 26, coordination has been handled in person, using a calendar printed each morning and distributed by hand. Attendance has never been higher.
