An Update on Our Exciting New Chapter

Reconstructed from a printout found in the Tube Room. Pending verification.

On the night of September 26, at 10:32 p.m., all of Carrefoogle’s data became permanently unavailable. Every customer record, every behavioral profile, every one of the 420 trillion catalogued human interactions — we no longer have them, and we do not know where they are. We want to be transparent with you, because transparency is one of our remaining assets: this was not part of a plan. But by 9:15 the next morning, it was.

Where some companies would see a catastrophe, our leadership saw the largest single-day privacy improvement in corporate history — and pivoted. Now we are a company with zero data, because we are fully committed to your privacy. Because you deserve clean.™

Effective immediately, Carrefoogle is once again a toothpaste company. Toothpaste is honest. Toothpaste is simple. Toothpaste cannot be accessed remotely.

A Carrefoogle Breakthrough

Normal Toothpaste™

Our most revolutionary product in a generation, and our first in 27 years to require no login. Our engineers asked a question nobody else in the industry dared to ask: what if toothpaste was just toothpaste? In a single weekend, they removed features it had taken us decades to build, until only the breakthrough remained: mint, calcium, and a tube.

Normal Toothpaste™ works offline. It works only offline. It does not learn your habits, does not know when you are home, and will not contact you. We have checked.
